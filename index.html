The reference analysis searches for μ- → e- (and e+) conversion with a cut-and-count. Its nominal selection is pyfitter's run1a. On an MDS pseudo-experiment it gives the sensitivity (pyfitter's CLs counting statistics, normalised with the MDS bookkeeping) and is validated against two independent implementations: RooCount (C++ port of the same selection) and Shape (an unbinned 2D fit with pyfitter's model). Producing this page

From RefAna. Only official runs are shown here: committed code, every event read, MC inputs, and pyCount, RooCount and Shape in agreement (result.json).

# from committed code; RooCount and Shape need a muse build of RefAna
bin/refana validate --official --pe /exp/mu2e/data/users/mu2epro/ensembles/MDS4/MDS4b/signal/CeMLeadingLog_1e-13/pe_1

# an official run is copied here automatically; an existing one with
bin/refana publish pyCount/runs/<run dir>

Outputs and provenance (git commit, cut profile, input checksums) are in pyCount/runs/<stamp>_<dataset>/; the files shown here are copied from there. Cut definitions: RefAna/config/cuts.yaml. Validation: RefAna/compare/README.md. To check your own analysis against the reference: refana score and refana explain (RefAna/README.md).
